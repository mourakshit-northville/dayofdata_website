# MITechCon 2026 — Oakland University — October 30, 2026

Upload original photographs from the three MITechCon speaking sessions here, preferably individual JPG, JPEG, PNG, or WebP images at their original quality. Large teaching/audience scenes are particularly useful.

How to upload:
1. On GitHub open this folder.
2. Select **Add file → Upload files**, and select or drag in the original photographs.
3. Choose **Commit changes** to the **main** branch.

Uploading files here does not automatically add them to the public gallery. After photos are uploaded, we will review them, add the event to the galleries of both websites, update the image manifests and captions, and verify production deployment.

- Portfolio gallery: https://www.mourakshit.com/gallery/
- Michigan community photo gallery: https://michigandataai.org/past-events.html

Gallery ordering: real photographs showing the biggest presentation/audience rooms first, then other presentation, smaller group, and networking photos. Do not display filenames in captions. Do not make synthetic or altered faces/audiences or post unrelated images.
