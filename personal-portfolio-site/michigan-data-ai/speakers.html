Speak with the community

Share something practical.

We welcome experienced speakers and first-time presenters across data engineering, databases, analytics, AI, BI, governance, cloud, architecture and career development.

Session formats

Several ways to contribute.

Lightning talk

10–15 minutes

A focused idea, demo, career lesson or technical pattern. Good for first-time speakers.

Technical session

30–60 minutes

Architecture, implementation, demo, case study or deep technical walkthrough.

Panel / workshop

Flexible

Discussion, hands-on learning, certification roadmap, student/career session or community roundtable.

Topics

Cross-platform by design.

The community is not limited to one vendor. We are especially interested in practical sessions that help people build, operate or understand real data and AI systems.

Data engineering & platformsDatabricks, Fabric, Spark, SQL, lakehouse, streaming, pipelines and data architecture.
AI & agentic systemsEnterprise agents, RAG, model routing, evaluation, governance, AI/BI and developer agents.
Analytics & BIPower BI, semantic models, conversational BI, metrics, visualization and decision support.
Career & communityCertification, student pathways, technical leadership, speaking, mentoring and workforce skills.
