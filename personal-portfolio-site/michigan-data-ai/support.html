Community support

Help keep local data and AI learning accessible.

Community contributions help cover food, beverages, venue needs, event supplies, student access, printing, speaker support and the operating costs behind free events.

Individual & community donations

Small contributions make free events possible.

Support can be financial or in-kind. A $25 contribution can help with refreshments or supplies; larger gifts can underwrite food, venues, speaker needs or an entire meetup.
