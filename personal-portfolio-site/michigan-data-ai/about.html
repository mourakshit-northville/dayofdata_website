About the community

A durable home for Michigan’s data and AI community.

Michigan Data & AI Community brings recurring learning, local user groups, conference programming, speaker development and community partnerships under one independent regional identity.

Mission

Make practical data and AI learning easier to access locally.

The community connects practitioners, students, educators, technology companies, employers and speakers across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan. The emphasis is practical education, peer exchange and a welcoming path into the regional technology community.
