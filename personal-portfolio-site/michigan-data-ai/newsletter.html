Publishing rhythm

A predictable newsletter, plus important event alerts.

The default cadence is a weekly community digest. Additional messages are reserved for registration openings, major schedule changes, capacity notices and time-sensitive event updates.

Weekly

Community Digest

Upcoming events, registration links, speaker news, resources and community highlights.

When needed

Event Alerts

Registration opens, venue changes, waitlists, final reminders and day-of-event information.

After events

Recap & Resources

Recordings, slides, photos, thank-yous, sponsor acknowledgements and the next event.

Newsletter format

Every issue follows the same simple structure.

1. From MouA short note from founder and lead organizer Mou Rakshit.
2. Next eventThe single most important upcoming event with a clear registration button.
3. Upcoming calendarThe next several community, Databricks and Day of Data events.
4. Community spotlightA speaker, volunteer, sponsor, university, student group or partner.
5. LearnOne or two recordings, articles, decks or certification resources.
6. Get involvedSpeak, volunteer, sponsor, donate or host a meetup.
