Privacy & data use

Your community relationship should be clear.

This page explains the intended handling of community mailing-list information and event-registration data.

Community mailing list

Joining the Michigan Data & AI Community mailing list is intended to be an explicit opt-in. The list may be used for community newsletters, upcoming event notices, calls for speakers, volunteer opportunities, sponsor-supported community announcements and related regional programs.

Event registration

Individual events may use Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup, Databricks Community, Sessionize or another event platform for registration, capacity, ticketing or check-in. Information submitted to those services is also subject to the privacy terms of the platform used for that event.

Newsletter consent

Registration for one event should not automatically be treated as consent for unrelated marketing. Where an attendee is offered an option to join the ongoing community mailing list, the signup should be clear and voluntary.

Unsubscribe and updates

Community newsletters will include a way to unsubscribe. Members may also contact the organizer to request an update or removal from the community mailing list.

Data minimization

The community should collect only information reasonably needed for event operations, community communication and program planning. Sensitive personal information is not required for ordinary community membership.

Questions

For questions about community data handling, contact mouraksh@umich.edu.

This is a community privacy notice for the current volunteer-led organization and is not legal advice. It can be revised as the organization, payment systems, nonprofit structure and mailing-list infrastructure mature.
