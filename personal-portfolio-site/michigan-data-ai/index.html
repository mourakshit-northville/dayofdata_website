Founder & lead organizer

Mou Rakshit

Mou Rakshit founded Michigan Data & AI Community to create an independent, year-round home for technical learning and community connection across Detroit, Ann Arbor and Southeast Michigan. She also founded and leads Day of Data Detroit and the Detroit Southeast Michigan Databricks User Group, and organizes the Southeast Michigan Data & AI Community.
