Why this community exists

Local learning should not depend on a single platform.

Michigan has strong technical communities, universities, employers and practitioners. This portal creates a durable home that can outlive individual event platforms and grow from free monthly meetups into larger workshops, conferences and regional partnerships.

01

Practical content

Sessions built around real architecture, engineering and delivery work.

02

Vendor-inclusive

Databricks, Microsoft, SQL, cloud, open source and emerging AI tools.

03

Accessible locally

Use free or low-cost venues whenever possible to keep community learning open.

04

Built to grow

A structure that can support sponsors, paid workshops and larger conferences later.
