Community model

A permanent local home that does not disappear when an event ends.

Michigan has strong technical communities, universities, employers and practitioners. This portal is being built as the durable home for recurring learning, member communication, event registration, sponsors, volunteers, speakers and future conferences.

01

Practical content

Architecture, engineering, analytics and AI sessions grounded in real work.

02

Vendor-inclusive

Databricks, Microsoft, SQL, cloud, open source and emerging AI tools.

03

Accessible locally

Free or low-cost venues and community support keep core learning open.

04

Built to grow

Monthly meetups today, workshops and larger regional conferences over time.

Your community relationship

Registration platforms can change. The community list should not.

Our goal is to keep the community website and mailing list as the continuing relationship with members. Event tools such as Luma, Eventbrite, Meetup and Databricks Community may be used when they make registration or discovery easier.

Newsletter consent will be explicit. We will not treat attendance at one event as automatic permission for unrelated marketing, and members will be able to unsubscribe from community email updates.
